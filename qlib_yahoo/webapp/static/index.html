Shared dictionary

    

    

    
    
Company fields group standard taxonomy families with blank segment and co-registrant context. Choose an observed unit and period before inserting.

    
Naming and search help
Company fields use a standard taxonomy family with blank segment/coreg context; this is not a promise of company coverage or data quality. Special dimensions, entities, custom tags and exact editions belong in Advanced definitions. Choices are exact observed unit/period recipes: currencies remain distinct, with no automatic FX conversion or computed TTM. Empty search shows starter concepts, not an exhaustive universe. Broad searches may return partial results within the search budget; concept groups and variant lists are not global counts. The large dictionary count measures stored definitions, not accounting concepts. Formula names use lowercase snake_case, such as sec_earnings_per_share_basic; some have shorter reviewed aliases. Original SEC tags retain their capitalization. Earlier canonical names remain usable, including special saved inputs. Advanced recipes retain raw definitions under Recipe details. Search runs on Search or Enter. Narrow the query or use an exact formula name. Preparation runs only when requested.
